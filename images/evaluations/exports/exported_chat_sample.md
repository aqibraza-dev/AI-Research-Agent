# Interactive Research Chat on Hydride Superconductors

## User

Can you summarize experimental evidence for high-pressure hydride superconductors?

## Assistant

Diamond anvil cell experiments on LaH10 confirm zero electrical resistance at 250K under 170 GPa pressure [1]. Subsequent magnetic susceptibility measurements confirm the Meissner effect [2].

## User

What are the remaining theoretical hurdles for room-temperature ambient pressure operation?

## Assistant

Stabilizing high electron-phonon coupling without megabar pressures requires ternary or quaternary hydride frameworks with high electronic density of states at the Fermi energy.
